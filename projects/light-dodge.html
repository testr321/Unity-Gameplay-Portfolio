Light Dodge

2D mobile survival game built for a Game Production module. A timer pool grows while you stay clear of obstacles and drains on hits, boundary bounces, or releasing input. That pool drives player scale and speed. Survival time is a separate score. Light bars spawn at the screen edges and drift across the arena.

My Contribution

Sole programmer. I designed the timer-pool pressure loop, spawn-delay ramp, tutorial, and mobile arena — 19 custom C# scripts on top of the Joystick Pack.

  • Core loop (LogicScript) — timer pool ticks up while collided == 0 and drains on hits, boundary bounces, and lost input; survival time is a separate score
  • Player (PlayerScript) — Rigidbody2D whose scale (timer * 0.08) and speed derive from the pool, with a shared collide counter so overlapping bars don’t leave a stale “touching” flag
  • Obstacles (LightSpawner, LightBarScript) — bars spawn at a random screen edge, drift, optionally rotate, and destroy past the far side; spawnDelay decays toward a floor every two seconds
  • Five-stage tap-gated tutorial with isolated LogicScriptTutorial / PlayerScriptTutorial copies, plus a resolution-independent arena (CameraSetups from ScreenToWorldPoint) and Joystick Pack input on the base Joystick class

Technical Breakdown

Problem

Hand-tuning difficulty didn’t hold, and the arena had to fit every mobile aspect ratio without per-device layout.

Solution

One timer pool drives scale and speed; spawnDelay ramps on a floor. Boundaries and the joystick are placed from ScreenToWorldPoint at startup. Overlapping bars share a collide count — clear only at zero.

Result

Staying alive makes you a bigger, slower target. Score is survival time in the clear. The same build places the arena correctly on any aspect ratio.

Reflection

What Went Well

  • One difficulty formula produced a tunable pressure curve
  • Joystick base-class abstraction kept input swappable
  • Staged onboarding got new players to a full round quickly

What I Would Redesign

  • Unify tutorial and main gameplay into one configurable stack
  • Replace static cross-scene state with a proper game manager
  • Object-pool light bars instead of instantiate/destroy per spawn
